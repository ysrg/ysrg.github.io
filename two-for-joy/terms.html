Eligibility

Two for Joy is for adults aged 18 and older. The app asks you to confirm this before continuing.

Reflection and entertainment

Two for Joy keeps a personal journal and offers reflective readings of dreams, signs, numbers and patterns. It does not predict events or provide medical, mental-health, legal, financial, safety or other professional advice. Do not use generated readings to make urgent decisions or replace qualified support.

AI-generated content

Google Gemini helps compose readings, reports and speech. Generated material can be incomplete or incorrect. Sky and number facts are computed and sourced folklore is used as context, but a reading remains a reflection rather than a statement about what will happen. Generation requires agreement to the privacy policy and is subject to network, provider and safety controls.

Your book

You keep ownership of the entries you create. Capture, export and deletion are not paywalled. Your book is stored locally with private iCloud backup when available; keep an export before changing devices. The developer cannot reconstruct your private journal.

Plus and one-off reports

Apple’s purchase screen shows the price, billing period and any introductory offer available to your Apple Account. Plus subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in Apple Account subscription settings. Any trial converts to the displayed recurring price unless cancelled. Existing subscribers and prior trial users may not qualify for a new introductory offer. The Year Ahead is a separate one-off purchase; a completed report is saved in your book. Temporary service or safety limits can pause generation.

Apple handles billing and refund requests. You can restore an eligible Plus subscription in the app. Cancellation does not delete your book. Consumable purchases and a deleted local report are not a general cloud archive provided by the developer.
