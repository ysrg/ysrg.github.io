Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Two for Joy is provided by ValandilTech. Contact: support@valandiltech.com.

Age confirmation

The 18+ confirmation is saved on your device. It is a self-declaration, separate from your profile birth date; it does not require an identity document.

Your local book and iCloud

Dreams, signs, numbers, moods, photos, readings, reports and profile details are stored in the app’s SwiftData book on your device. Private CloudKit backup may restore the book to your Apple devices. There is no Two for Joy account. The developer cannot browse or recover your private iCloud book.

Generation requests

When you request a reading, Today’s generated content, a report or speech, the app sends the material needed for that request to the Two for Joy backend and Google Gemini. This can include journal text, a photo you choose for a reading, derived patterns, sky/number facts, and report profile/context. The backend does not write journal text, photos, reading prose, reports or audio to its database. Spoken audio and generated results are cached on the device. Concurrent duplicate requests may briefly share the same result in server memory.

Google processing and retention

The production Gemini service uses a billing-enabled Google project. Under Google’s paid-service terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google’s products. Google nevertheless retains prompts, context and outputs for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring and policy enforcement. When search grounding is used, Google also retains its prompts, context and output for 30 days to support that feature. These provider retention periods are separate from the Two for Joy backend’s no-content-storage policy.

Speech requests disable optional Interactions API conversation-state storage. This does not disable Google’s abuse-monitoring retention. See Google’s abuse-monitoring policy and retention documentation.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat verifies receipts and manages Plus access using a randomly generated app installation id, product/transaction identifiers and purchase history. The developer receives entitlement and transaction information, not your payment-card details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Notifications and operational records

If you enable notifications, the server stores the APNs device token, time zone, wake hour and anonymous app id to deliver them. It also keeps quotas, usage/safety counters, purchase-consumption records and an entitlement mirror. Operational logs contain counts, hashes and provider status rather than journal text or photos. Aggregate analytics count interactions such as opening Today, keeping an entry or completing a purchase; they do not contain the entry itself. Operational SQLite data is backed up to a private Cloudflare R2 bucket.

Tracking

Two for Joy does not use advertising identifiers or track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising.

Voice and photos

Dream transcription takes place on the device. The app does not send your microphone recording to the Two for Joy backend. Photos are sent for generation only when you request a photo reading; otherwise they remain in your local/private iCloud book.

Your choices

Existing readers can defer updated generation terms and continue local capture, export and deletion. In You, export a copy or use Delete everything to remove the book and request removal of operational rows tied to the current anonymous id. Global service totals contain no journal content and are retained. Apple, RevenueCat, Google and backup systems can retain transaction/security records for their own retention periods or legal obligations. Contact support about operational data requests; do not email your journal.

Changes

Material changes to processing disclosures are presented for agreement before generated features are used again. The current policy is published on this page.
