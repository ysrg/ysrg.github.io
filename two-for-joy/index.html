Two for Joy · 18+

Keep the mark.
Let the book remember.

A daily almanac for dreams, signs, numbers, moods and the small patterns that keep returning.

Keep a dream before it fades. Write down a sign, a coincidence or a number. The augur offers a reflective reading grounded in sourced folklore; the moon and number facts are computed, and patterns come from your own entries.

Your book stays yours

Your journal lives on your device, with private iCloud backup when available. There is no Two for Joy login. Capture, export and deletion remain available without Plus.

Written and spoken readings

One reading each day is included for free. Plus adds more readings, dream readings, monthly pattern pages and spoken readings. The Year Ahead is a separate one-off purchase. Current regional prices and any eligible trial appear in the app and Apple’s purchase confirmation.

When you request generated content, the needed material is sent to the Two for Joy server and Google Gemini. See the privacy policy for processing and retention details.

Two for Joy is for reflection and entertainment. It does not give professional advice or predict real-world outcomes.

The iPhone app is being prepared for its first App Store release.
