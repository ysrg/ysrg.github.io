Two for Joy support

For purchase questions, a problem with the app or feedback, email support@valandiltech.com. Include your app version and a description of the problem. Please do not send your private journal or full payment details.

Restore Plus

Open the You tab, open the Plus offer and choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple manages subscriptions and billing.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your Apple Account name, then Subscriptions and Two for Joy. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues through the period shown by Apple.

Export or delete your book

Open You to export your book or use Delete everything. Deletion removes the book from the app’s local and private CloudKit storage and requests removal of server operational rows tied to the current anonymous app id. Apple and service providers can retain records required for purchases, security or their retention policies.

What if my book is missing?

Check that iCloud is available on the device and uses the same Apple Account. Your book is local-first with private iCloud backup; support cannot read or reconstruct the contents of your journal. Export a copy before changing devices.

A delayed reading

Keep the entry in your book and try again later. A temporary model, network or usage-limit error does not remove the entry. If a purchase succeeded but its result is unavailable, contact support before purchasing it again.

Refunds

Apple handles payment and refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.
