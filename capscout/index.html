Frequently Asked Questions

What does CapScout do?
CapScout analyzes SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 20-F) using AI to generate summaries, compare year-over-year changes, identify quarterly trends, and compare companies against their peers.

What's included in the Standard subscription?
Filing summaries, year-over-year comparisons, quarterly trend analysis, and the Magic 8-Ball verdict feature.

What's included in the Pro subscription?
Everything in Standard, plus peer comparison analysis and Deep Research mode with comprehensive multi-source analysis.

Is the Magic 8-Ball feature free?
Yes, the Magic 8-Ball verdict feature is free for all users without requiring sign-in.

Is this investment advice?
No. CapScout provides analysis for educational purposes only. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.

How do I cancel my subscription?
You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple ID settings on your device.
