Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 20, 2025

Introduction

CapScout ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique identifier for your account. If you choose to share your email address or name during sign-in, we store that information as well.

Usage Data: We track your subscription status and feature usage to provide our service.

Research Queries: When you use Deep Research features, your questions and the AI-generated responses may be stored to provide query history functionality.

Saved Items: Research reports you save to your Library are stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To process your in-app purchases
  • To provide query history for Deep Research features
  • To improve our service

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Apple Sign In: For authentication
  • Google Cloud (Gemini AI): To generate analysis and research reports
  • SEC EDGAR: To fetch public company filings
  • Apple App Store: To process in-app purchases and subscriptions

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Query history is retained while you maintain an active Pro subscription. You can request deletion of your account by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 12. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 12.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
